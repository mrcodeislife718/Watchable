# Watchable TV — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Commercial streaming television platform

Watchable is complete only when content/catalog ingestion, playback, entitlement, account/billing, device support, reliability, and customer operations work in production and support the intended business model.

Required proof: end-to-end playback and entitlement flows; tests for auth, subscriptions, catalog state, device sessions, buffering/error recovery, and concurrency; failure tests for CDN/provider/network/device/payment outages; benchmarks for startup time, rebuffering, playback success, API latency, concurrency, and cost; security/DRM/rights controls as applicable; repeatable web/mobile/TV deployment; real household usage; paid conversion, churn, support burden, and unit economics.

Marketing claims require exact playback, deployment, usage, or commercial evidence.

**Next proof target:** execute a production-faithful household journey from signup/payment through entitlement and playback across representative devices, including network degradation and provider failure, while collecting QoE and recovery metrics.
